Face Jump

Run and jump through 4 worlds — Grassland, Desert, Caves and Castle.
Lean or turn your head left/right to run.
Open your mouth to jump — keep it open to jump higher.
Pucker your lips to blow a bubble that traps enemies and stuns bosses.
Beat the boss at the end of each world (stomp its head!) to open the gate to the flag.

Power-ups hide in some ? blocks: ★ star invincible for 8 s · 🪶 feather double jump for 15 s · ♥ extra life.
Red spiky slimes can't be stomped — bubble them. Bats fly in the Caves and Castle.
Face: sit centered, relaxed, and hold still for a second to calibrate. Small head moves = stand still.
Keyboard: ←→/AD run · Space/↑ jump (hold = higher) · X bubble
