Face Arcade

Five games you play with your face and hands. Grab a webcam, pick a game, and use your head, mouth or hands as the controller.

Face Pilot

1 player

Space shooter. Dodge asteroids, blast them apart and grab gold stars.

  • Move head → steer the ship
  • Open mouth → fire lasers
  • Pucker lips → shield bubble
Play →

Face Jump

1 player

Mario-style platformer. Run, stomp slimes, bump ? blocks for coins and reach the flag.

  • Lean / turn head → run left or right
  • Open mouth → jump (keep open = higher)
  • Pucker lips → blow a bubble that traps slimes
Play →

Face Pong

2 players

Head-to-head pong with two faces on one webcam — or solo against the computer.

  • Move head up / down → move your paddle
  • Open mouth at impact → power shot
  • Pucker lips at impact → curve shot
Play →

Hand Ninja

2 players

Fruit-slicing duel. Both players get the same fruit — slice the most before time runs out, dodge the bombs.

  • Index finger → your blade
  • Swipe fast → slice fruit, 3+ in one swipe = combo
  • Mouse / touch mode for solo play
Play →

Hand Hockey

2 players

Air hockey where your hand is the mallet — head-to-head on one webcam, or solo against the computer.

  • Move your hand → move your mallet
  • Swing into the puck → harder shot
  • Keyboard mode: WASD vs arrows
Play →

Works best in Chrome or Edge with good lighting. Every game also has a keyboard or mouse mode.
Face and hand tracking run entirely in your browser — no video is uploaded.
