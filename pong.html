Face Pong

Two faces, one webcam. Sit side by side —
left player moves the left paddle, right player the right one.
Move your head up/down to move your paddle.
Open your mouth as the ball hits your paddle for a power shot!
Pucker your lips as it hits for a curve shot (once per rally).
Solo? Fight through a tournament of 5 computer bosses, each with its own trick.

Hit the ball through a glowing power orb to win it: ⇕ big paddle · ●● multi-ball · ❄ freeze opponent · ▮ goal guard (saves one goal).
Webcam: both players relax and hold still for a second to calibrate. Sit far enough back that both faces fit in the preview.
Keyboard: P1 W/S move, D power, A curve · P2 ↑/↓ move, ← power, → curve
Video stays on your computer; nothing is uploaded.
