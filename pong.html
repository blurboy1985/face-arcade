Face Pong

Two faces, one webcam. Sit side by side —
left player moves the left paddle, right player the right one.
Move your head up/down to move your paddle.
Open your mouth as the ball hits your paddle for a power shot!

Webcam: both players hold still for a second to calibrate. Sit far enough back that both faces fit in the preview.
Keyboard: P1 W/S move, D power · P2 ↑/↓ move, ← power
Video stays on your computer; nothing is uploaded.
